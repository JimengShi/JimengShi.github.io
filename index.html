Introduction

Hi there! I am a postdoctoral research associate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), working with Prof. Shaowen Wang and Prof. Jiawei Han. I obtained my PhD in Computer Science from Florida International University (FIU), advised by Prof. Giri Narasimhan. I was a Research Fellow at Columbia University. My research focuses on advancing AI for Science with a specific goal on environmental sustainability.

Sincere gratitude to funding sources: NSF I-GUIDE led by UIUC, NSF LEAP led by Columbia U.

[Job Hunting] I am on the job market for research positions in both academia and industry. If you believe I might be a good fit, please feel free to reach out at jimengshi008@gmail.com.

Research Interests

  • AI for Natural Science
    • Environment: spatio-temporal forecasting: (flood prediction WaLeF, ReFine), forecasting-optimization (flood mitigation FIDLAr), explainable AI (TimeX++).
    • Climate: diffusion model for weather prediction (CoDiCast), foundation models for weather (Survey)
  • LLM, RAG, Foundation Model
    • Retrieval and Structuring: Structured RAG (HyperCube-RAG, MultiCube-RAG, TaSR-RAG)
    • In-domain Foundation Models: Benchmark (Water-Bench), Retrieval-Augmention Forecast (Water-RAF)
  • Agentic AI & Reinforcement Learning
    • Multi-Agent: MultiCube-RAG, PyRAG
    • Reinforcement Learning: PyRAG
